How we help prevent crime

Today, most traditional home security systems will trigger an alarm when an intruder is breaking into your home, but by then it may be too late. Now, you can feel safer than ever with SimpliSafe® Active Guard Outdoor Protection — our AI-powered Outdoor Camera and Video Doorbell work with live agents to proactively deter intruders outside your home.

FAQs

Why do I need professional monitoring? Isn’t the siren enough to scare away burglars?Why do I need professional monitoring? Isn’t the siren enough to scare away burglars?
We strongly recommend a monitoring plan to get the most protection from your security system. Professional monitoring doesn’t just request police dispatch when you’re unable to, but fire and medical services as well. With select professional monitoring plans and the Outdoor Camera (Series 2), SimpliSafe® Active Guard Outdoor Protection delivers a double layer of defense — advanced AI technology combined with live security agents — to help keep intruders outside your home and prevent crime before it happens.
What makes SimpliSafe® professional monitoring different?What makes SimpliSafe® professional monitoring different?
SimpliSafe's new Active Guard Outdoor Protection introduces a revolutionary double-layer approach to home security, combining the power of artificial intelligence (AI) technology with a team of expert monitoring professionals to help prevent crime before it happens. The service, which builds upon our recently launched intruder intervention*, extends the technology to outside the home. Now, when your system is armed during plan hours and motion is detected outside, agents can act to deter crimes in progress. They can see and speak directly to potential intruders in real time using the live feed and 2-way audio, and trigger camera sirens — all while capturing video evidence of what’s happening.
SimpliSafe® Active Guard Outdoor Protection and intruder intervention are available on select professional monitoring plans only.
These services are available through select camera hardware and require service opt in.
*Formerly known as 24/7 live guard protection.
Will the police automatically come to my house during an alarm?Will the police automatically come to my house during an alarm?
Monitoring agents will attempt to contact you first via Alarm Text and phone call. If you’ve set off the alarm by mistake, you can easily cancel via the app, Alarm Text, or by giving our agent your safeword over the phone prior to our agents requesting emergency dispatch. If you can’t be reached during an alarm, agents will process the alarm as a real emergency, contacting 911 centers to request police dispatch. With select professionally monitored plans, our agents can verify threats using video evidence, which can mean priority dispatch and faster police response.*
*Vs. non-verified SimpliSafe® alarms. Video verification available with select professional monitoring plans.
How does SimpliSafe protect my privacy?How does SimpliSafe protect my privacy?
We are committed to protecting our customers' privacy and sensitive data as vigilantly as their homes. First, you have complete control over when live agents monitor your location and which cameras they use. Agents only have access to outdoor enabled cameras during a motion event that occurs during your subscription service hours when your system is armed. And indoors, agents only have access to the Smart Alarm indoor camera during an alarm (and only if you’ve enabled the intruder intervention feature.
In addition, we use physical, technical and administrative controls to protect the confidentiality and security of biometric information which includes requiring agents to pass extensive background checks and work in a secured location.
For more information, visit our Privacy Promise page.
Is there a catch when you say “no contract” for monitoring plans?Is there a catch when you say “no contract” for monitoring plans?
SimpliSafe monitoring subscribers are not tied up in long-term contracts. Our plans are billed monthly, and you can cancel at any time.
If I install it myself, can I still get professional monitoring?If I install it myself, can I still get professional monitoring?
Yes. Regardless of whether you install your system yourself or select Pro Install, you can still choose the monitoring plan that best meets your needs.
Can small businesses use SimpliSafe® Active Guard Outdoor Protection?Can small businesses use SimpliSafe® Active Guard Outdoor Protection?
This service is designed for residential use, not commercial use.
What are the outdoor monitoring options?What are the outdoor monitoring options?
We offer two professionally monitored plans that include all the benefits of the Core plan plus Active Guard Outdoor Protection. The Pro plan includes overnight outdoor protection (8pm to 6am local time), and the Pro Plus plan includes 24/7 outdoor protection.
What products are compatible with SimpliSafe® Active Guard Outdoor Protection?What products are compatible with SimpliSafe® Active Guard Outdoor Protection?
SimpliSafe Active Guard Outdoor Protection works with the Video Doorbell (Series 2) and Outdoor Camera (Series 2) — you can add up to 3 cameras total, in any combination of the two. The Outdoor Camera can run on battery, but Active Guard Outdoor Protection requires it to stay plugged in with the included power cable for the service to work. The Video Doorbell (Series 2) is powered through your existing wired setup.
Do live monitoring agents interact with people on my property?Do live monitoring agents interact with people on my property?
Monitoring agents are trained to observe any person on your property and potentially engage them if they notice suspicious behavior. When you subscribe to a monitoring plan with SimpliSafe Active Guard Outdoor Protection and your system is armed within plan hours, agents are able to interact with people outside of your home. Where permitted by law, you can use the SimpliSafe App to save familiar faces of friends and family for agents to ignore. If the person isn't recognized, an agent may engage to assess any possible threat. The agent can watch, assist or deter people by using the outdoor camera's two-way communication and/or siren. For everyday activity such as deliveries, the agent will monitor the delivery person across your enrolled cameras but will not engage unless they observe something suspicious.
