Temperature Sensor

Detects extreme temperatures.

Our Temperature Sensor triggers an alarm if your home drops below 41°F, so you can prevent pipes from freezing or bursting. You can also set an upper temperature limit to protect things like pets, paintings and plants. With professional monitoring, we notify you immediately if there’s an alarm.

Features

  • Wireless

  • Peel & stick installation

  • Lower & upper temperature limits

  • Install near thermostat or exposed plumbing that's vulnerable to freeze

  • Access to hourly readings

  • 1 CR-2032 Lithium 3V battery (included)

  • Up to 5 year battery (depending on usage)

  • 1.5 x 2.25 x 0.62 inches
